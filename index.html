Settings & privacy

Install on your phone

On iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android: Chrome → menu → Install app / Add to Home screen. Requires HTTPS hosting.

Reminders

Browser notifications depend on your device and installation. This web edition doesn't provide reliable scheduled reminders while closed.

Apple Health & Samsung Health

Not connected in the web edition. Direct Apple Health / Android Health Connect access requires native app builds and platform permissions. Samsung Health may share compatible records with Health Connect if configured. No health data is read or uploaded here.

Your data

Saved locally in this browser. Clearing browser storage removes it. No account or cloud sync.
